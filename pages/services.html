Our Services

All services include personalized attention, premium amenities, and our signature Haven Retreat hospitality.

Gourmet Breakfast

Farm-to-table breakfast at The Canopy restaurant using produce from our organic gardens. Seasonal menus, vegan & gluten-free options available.

  • Complimentary for all guests
  • Served 7:00 AM – 10:30 AM
  • In-room breakfast available

Spa & Wellness Centre

Our award-winning 3,000 sq ft spa offers treatments rooted in ancient healing traditions using organic, locally-sourced products.

  • Swedish & deep tissue massage
  • Hydrotherapy pool & steam room
  • Couples treatment suites

Personal Concierge

Your dedicated concierge is available 24/7 to curate bespoke experiences – from island tours to helicopter charters and surprise arrangements.

  • Available 24 hours a day
  • Local tours & activity bookings
  • Special occasion planning

Infinity Pool & Beach Club

Our stunning heated infinity pool is open year-round. Enjoy private cabanas, poolside cocktails, and direct beach access with sun loungers.

  • Heated pool, open year-round
  • Private beach cabanas
  • Poolside bar & light bites

Fine Dining – The Canopy

An intimate dining experience on an open-air terrace. Chef Marco's ever-evolving tasting menus celebrate the finest local produce and seasonal flavours.

  • 3-course & 7-course menus
  • Curated wine pairings
  • Private dining room

Yoga & Mindfulness

Daily sunrise yoga on our open-air pavilion, plus evening meditation walks, breathwork sessions, and private mindfulness coaching for all levels.

  • Daily sunrise yoga (7:00 AM)
  • Evening meditation sessions
  • Private coaching available
