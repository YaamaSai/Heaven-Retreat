Our Story

A sanctuary born from a love of nature, art, and genuine hospitality.

Our Vision

Where Luxury Meets Nature

Founded in 2014 by the Whitmore family, Haven Retreat was built on one simple belief: that true luxury is not about opulence alone, but about feeling completely at peace. Nestled among ancient trees and ocean breezes, our retreat was designed to be a living, breathing sanctuary.

Every corner of Haven Retreat tells a story � from the hand-carved furniture sourced from local artisans, to the organic gardens that supply our kitchen each morning. We believe hospitality is an art form, and we practice it with quiet dedication every single day.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Sustainability

We operate with a deep commitment to the environment. From solar energy to zero-waste kitchens, every decision is guided by respect for our natural surroundings.

Authenticity

We celebrate local culture, partnering with nearby artisans, farmers, and guides to create experiences that are genuinely rooted in our location.

Excellence

We pursue perfection in every detail from the thread count of our linens to the timing of your morning coffee, nothing is left to chance.

Warmth

Behind every great hotel is a great team. Our staff are trained not just in service, but in empathy because we believe every guest deserves to feel truly welcomed.

Our Journey

Milestones

2014

The Beginning

Haven Retreat opens its doors with just 12 rooms � a passion project of the Whitmore family, designed by award-winning architect Elena Vasquez.

2016

First Award

Recognized as "Best Boutique Hotel in the Region" by Luxury Travel Magazine. The spa wing is inaugurated with four treatment rooms.

2019

The Expansion

We expanded to 48 rooms, adding the iconic Haven Penthouse and Ocean View Suites. Our signature farm-to-table restaurant, The Canopy, is launched.

2022

Green Certification

Haven Retreat becomes the first hotel in the valley to achieve Platinum Green Certification, running 100% on renewable energy.

2026

Today & Beyond

With over 15,000 happy guests and a 4.9⭐ Google rating, we continue to redefine what it means to offer a boutique luxury experience.
